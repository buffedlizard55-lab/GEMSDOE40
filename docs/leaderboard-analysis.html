LEADERBOARD ANALYSIS / MEASURED, NOT SPECULATED

Why 0.2778 happened.

Byte-exact set comparison plus the metric’s own marginal economics. The score attribution itself remains owner-reported.

The measured facts

  • H33-B2 is exactly H27-4 (40,199 px) minus the 2,545 pixels at ≤200 m from the catalogue; it adds zero pixels (measured JSON).
  • DTI = T/(0.2T + 0.2FP + 0.8G). A removal losing L of TP credit and saving S of FP mass helps iff (1−0.2D)·L < 0.2D·S. At D=0.2778 the bound is 0.05883 TP credit per FP unit (~17:1).
  • The owner reports 0.2708 → 0.2778 for this prune; the leaderboard does not identify TIFFs, so the pairing stays unauthenticated.
  • Session-2 measurements on local instruments: H13 CAT-HID 0.021464, H8 0.023673, H33-B2 0.003590; proxy pooled DTI H33-B2 0.091550.

What could beat it (hypotheses, not forecasts)

  1. More placement, same budget. Live gains in the 0.24–0.28 family came from pruning and trace arrangement, not new physics. Placing mass on actual hidden-trace geometry is the lever — unmeasurable locally, so the next session targets blind-fault physics (H9) where placement evidence is independent of the catalogue.
  2. Concentrate value. H13’s 5.8× over H8 shows emission form matters; a future candidate should never max-normalize diluted flanks.
  3. Corroborate before emitting. Each additional independent family (gravity, geodetic, seismic, basement depth) that must agree shrinks the false-positive class the metric charges.
  4. Keep mass near the working budget. 21–44k positive pixels with trace-like arrangement; diffuse fields over 100k px have never scored above 0.19 live in the corpus.
