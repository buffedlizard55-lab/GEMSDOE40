Scientific target

Faults—not vents, springs, or reservoirs.

The DOE GEMS Prize Challenge asks for geological faults that may indicate geothermal resources. A potential-field contact is not automatically a fault; a mapped fault is not automatically permeable; and neither establishes a viable reservoir. GEMSDOE40 uses Euler depth-deconvolution and multi-height persistence as source-location hypotheses, not as direct geothermal-site predictions.

The registered scientific ideas were: (1) magnetic–gravity depth consensus; (2) upward-continuation persistence in TMI Euler solutions; and (3) focal-mechanism-constrained orientation. H1 and strict H2 stopped at feasibility; H2-B failed the proxy gate. On 2026-10-06 the register was extended with five H40 hypotheses: the SI = 0 Euler depth-cluster crest (implemented — does not pay), a magnetic × gravity depth conjunction (the cheapest next test), strike-continuation on the catalogue flanks (refuted by the paired live measurement: deleting 6,436 flank dots raised the live score 0.2600 → 0.2778), depth-sliced emission (noise-level change), and a hidden-truth density inversion from the public score record (not yet implemented).

How to fix “Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”

The portal reads the raster it is given, so the fix is a property of the file, not of the upload flow. Two mechanisms produce the error in this programme and both are prevented the same way:

  1. A float32 sentinel inside the footprint. The organizer feature rasters fill cells outside the study area with −3.4028234663852886e+38; three thousand and sixty-one of those cells fall inside the sample mask. Any pipeline that treats the raw band as data and re-emits it writes values far below 0.
  2. NaN inside the footprint. NaN is legal only outside the sample mask. Writing NaN where the sample has data (or writing 0 outside where the sample has NaN) also fails.

The fix, in three lines of policy: (i) build the emission on the sample's finite mask only; (ii) write exactly 1.0 where a dot is emitted and 0.0 elsewhere inside that mask, with nodata = None, so every in-footprint value is finite and inside [0, 1]; (iii) emit the two twins — the portal-safe one with zeros outside the footprint, and the sample-convention one with NaN outside — and verify by reading the written file back. scripts/run_euler_depth_cluster.py asserts all three conditions before it exits, and the 2026-10-06 artifact passes them (5,167,373/5,167,373 in-footprint values finite and in range; 7,111,787 NaN cells outside in the twin). Passing this check is necessary and not sufficient: it says nothing about scientific quality.
