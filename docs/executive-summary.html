Scientific target and method

Faults—not vents, springs, or reservoirs.

The DOE GEMS Prize Challenge asks for geological faults relevant to geothermal resources. A potential-field contact is not automatically a fault; a mapped fault is not automatically permeable; and neither establishes a viable reservoir.

H7 estimates source locations and depths with a magnetic RTP SI=0 Euler solve. It weights accepted source points by a bounded, continuous gravity-horizontal-gradient context factor, clusters the solutions in 3-D, and projects the weighted solution cloud through a Gaussian KDE. It is not gradient thresholding. The gradient is only a soft context feature; gravity Euler depth solutions were not produced for H7.

Four distinct hypotheses were registered for this cycle. H4, the higher-confidence dual-field depth-consensus candidate, stopped before proxy scoring because raw-gravity SI=−1 had no solutions within its locked depth gate. H7 was registered as a lower-confidence fallback before its code/output was written. H5 and H6 remain untried. No gate was relaxed.

Future upload guide

No candidate is cleared today.

Do not upload H7, H40, H2-B, or the historical H40-4 TIFF. The following checklist is for a future candidate only, after it passes every preregistered scientific, format, and uniqueness gate. No submission name or note is supplied for an ineligible file.
  1. Confirm the dated validation report says eligible for human review and names the exact candidate hash. A format pass or uniqueness pass alone is insufficient.
  2. Confirm the candidate beats the then-current comparable holdout incumbent under the locked spatial-block rule, with at least 18/24 wins and no block loss worse than −0.005. Keep proxy results clearly distinguished from official scores.
  3. Confirm the full pinned prior cache is complete and raw/pixel comparisons pass against every same-grid raster in the then-current inventory.
  4. Download the exact TIFF linked by the cleared report. Do not resave, reproject, compress, clip, or otherwise change it after validation.
  5. Only then open the official DrivenData submissions page ↗, upload once, and use the name/comment supplied specifically for that candidate. This site will not invent a submission label.
  6. Retain the portal's actual receipt and file association. Do not infer upload success or TIFF-level score attribution from a public account row.

Format gate

Read back the TIFF and compare it to the sample: one float32 band, exact grid/transform/CRS, finite in-footprint [0,1] values, and NaN outside. A portal range error is prevented by this technical check; technical validity does not establish score quality.
