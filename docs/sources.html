  • Two proxy bytes: the current and historical owner mirrors differ in 1,450 cells (Pearson 0.991230; positive-support Jaccard 0.982655). Same-candidate scores remain tied to their respective hashes. See the dated reconciliation.
  • Sample irregularity: the sample/template has 60,988 exact known-label positive pixels inside the footprint despite sibling prose describing an all-zero sample. It is used only for shape, transform, CRS, and footprint—not as truth.
  • Data-access boundary: the official competition data/submission pages require login. No private test labels or organizer receipts were available during this local analysis.
  • Prior-output boundary: the refreshed scan contains public TIFF files from 55 sibling-repository heads, not a verified official submission ledger. Paths and hashes do not prove that an artifact was uploaded, scored, selected, or linked to any leaderboard account.
