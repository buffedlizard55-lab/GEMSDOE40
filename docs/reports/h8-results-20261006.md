## What H8 is

Analytic-signal source imaging instead of Euler least squares. For TMI (and for the first vertical derivative of isostatic gravity as a local top-edge approximation), the amplitude `A = sqrt(Tx²+Ty²+Tz²)` peaks over magnetization/density contrast boundaries independent of magnetization direction (Nabighian 1972; Roest et al. 1992). The contact-calibrated local-wavenumber depth `z = sqrt(A/|λ_min(Hessian(A))|)` labels every amplitude-qualified cell with an effective source depth (SPI family; Thurston & Smith 1997). Dense depth-consistent cells are weighted by shallowness, upward-continuation persistence, local solution density, local depth scatter, and corroboration against the committed H4 Euler cloud, then Gaussian-KDE-smoothed into a continuous [0,1] field with a soft catalogue-flank ramp.
