## 2. What was computed

* Euler deconvolution (Reid et al. 1990, SI = 0 contact form with offset A) on `rtp`, `tmi` and `iso_grav_anom` at windows [10, 16, 24] px, stride 4 px: **356,650** depth-labelled solutions (per window: 10 px → 125,566, 16 px → 115,720, 24 px → 115,364).
* Per-window depth-cluster KDE (σ = 1.7 px; weights = shallowness × quality × tightness × depth consistency × magnetic/gravity concordance), combined by geometric mean across all three windows: **2,187,859** cells supported at every scale out of 3,392,595 supported by at least one.
* Mean Euler depth per window is carried into the ranker, so the ranking sees the depth structure, not only the density.
* Ranker: spatially blocked (5 × 6), out-of-fold L2 logistic discriminant over 27 features — the three KDE fields, the scale-stable field, the three depth maps, potential-field gradients, DEM gradient and two-scale curvature, ten competition bands, two cross-field gradient ratios, an RTP short-scale curvature, a local radiometric z-score and the distance to the provided catalogue — trained on public SGMC faults absent from the provided catalogue. **Out-of-fold AUC 0.7488** pooled, 0.7615 mean over 20 blocks.
* Emission: Poisson-disk value-ranked thinning at 3 px spacing (the metric's own R = 300 m), restricted to the Euler-gated support (2,101,752 cells, catalogue flank 1 px), mass chosen by the recalibrated instrument: **40,000 dots**, w = 0.0987.
