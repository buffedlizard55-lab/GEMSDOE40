# Three-pass review — H41 release, 6 October 2026 (second sitting)

The brief requires three passes: **Pass 1 implement**, **Pass 2 review bugs and edge cases**,
**Pass 3 re-check every requirement against its evidence**. This is the record of all three for
the H41 release. Nothing here is a competition score; every number traces to a receipt.

The pipeline: 356,650 SI = 0 contact solutions (three fields × three windows, stride 4) →
per-window depth-cluster KDE → geometric-mean multi-scale stability (2,187,859 cells) →
Euler-gated support (2,101,752 cells, catalogue flank 1 px) → spatially blocked (5 × 6)
out-of-fold L2 logistic discriminant over 27 features trained on public SGMC faults missing
from the provided catalogue (pooled out-of-fold **AUC 0.7488**) → 3-px Poisson-disk
value-ranked thinning at a mass chosen by the recalibrated instrument (**40,000 dots**,
w = 0.0987) → all-finite `float32` GeoTIFF, NaN twin, zip.

## Irregularities flagged, not hidden

1. **The promotion bar is not met.** Instrument LOO Spearman 0.705 < 0.80, and H40-E (a prior,
   never-scored Euler arm) leads the blocked proxy mean. The site therefore carries
   `HOLD — DO NOT SUBMIT` on this repository's gate while still offering the download.
2. **The surrogate is anti-correlated with the live scores** out of fold (whole-map proxy DTI,
   LOO Spearman −0.897). Any claim that optimising the SGMC surrogate improves the leaderboard
   score is contradicted by this repository's own measurements.
3. **Live scores are owner-reported.** Public account rows do not authenticate which TIFF earned
   which score; all 16 anchors carry that label in every receipt.
4. **Instrument bias.** The saturating model under-predicts the two best anchors by ≈0.09; the
   report states the possible implication and labels it as one extrapolation, not a forecast.
5. **Unreachable official sources.** USGS/ScienceBase/OpenEI hosts are unreachable from the
   sandbox shell; H42 and H44 are registered as blocked on that basis with direct links.
6. **Browser QA is CI-only.** Chromium cannot be downloaded here, so the four copy controls, the
   real download bytes and the no-overflow checks are verified by the same script in CI, not in
   this sandbox; the static contract is verified locally by `pytest`.
7. **The board moved past the brief.** The brief's 0.3195 was rank 4 of a four-row observation on
   2026-10-06; the leading value in the same observation is 0.3345. Nothing here claims any
   organizer score.
