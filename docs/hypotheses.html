What H8 measured, and what it teaches

  • Synthetic truth first. Contact depths recovered within 20% (z0 ≥ 800 m), edges localized ≤1 px, SI bias documented (pole → z/√2), noise tolerance 30% — all before a real-data number existed.
  • Novelty is real. Max |Pearson| 0.2528 across 343 byte-verified prior outputs; the closest relative is this repo's own earlier Euler submission. ASA/SPI depth cells are a new pattern in the corpus.
  • The gate failed on mass and instrument fit. 155k confidence units with TP_w ≈ 2,205 vs FP_w ≈ 145,742 on the proxy; the catalogue-flank ramp (motivated by the owner's measured pruning win) removes mass exactly where the catalogue-like proxy lives. The two instruments ask different questions — again.
  • Catalogue DTI cannot validate sparse discovery. A uniform random field (0.1856) beats every targeted construction on the areal catalogue, as H4-A also found. Coverage ≠ discovery.

Why 0.2778 is the owner's best — and the path beyond it

H33-B2 = H27-4 base minus 2,545 pixels at ≤200 m from the catalogue (measured set equality). DTI's α=0.2 makes off-target mass cheap to delete; hidden truth cannot sit on the catalogue, so flank mass is nearly pure false positive. That was a pruning win. To go further you must add correctly placed new geometry — which needs a placement instrument validated on something other than the circular proxy: e.g., expert-adopted synthetic faults injected off-catalogue, or a geologically held-out subregion with independent mapping. Register that protocol before the next model search.

What is novel here — and what isn't

The analytic signal, local-wavenumber depth and the SPI family are established geophysics (Nabighian 1972; Roest et al. 1992; Thurston & Smith 1997; Blakely 1995). The bounded claim is a specific construction — tapered FFT derivatives, Hessian-of-A contact depth, persistence/density/consistency weighting, Euler corroboration, flank ramp — and a raster proven distinct from the dated public corpus.
