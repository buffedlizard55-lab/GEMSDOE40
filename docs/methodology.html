METHOD / SESSION 2 PIPELINE

Euler depths → trace consensus → map.

Every stage frozen before scoring. The solver settings are session 1’s H4 settings; session 2 changed only the emission geometry.

  1. Input cloud (frozen H4). Reid SI=0 contact equation with arbitrary offset A on TMI (200 m continuation) and gravity dG/dz (top-edge approximation), 10–51 px windows, stride 5; QC by condition number, relative residual, depth 100–8,000 m, offset and depth-se gates; 3-D cluster weights penalizing depth, scatter and cross-window inconsistency. 46,656 records; 20,617 weighted.
  2. Per-family KDE. Bilinear splat of weighted points, Gaussian σ=2, truncate 4, 99.5-percentile normalization.
  3. Consensus field. C = (M + G + √(MG))/3 — cross-family corroboration; exact known pixels zeroed.
  4. Orientation. Structure tensor (inner σ=1, integration σ=3); isotropy floor 0.15; gradient direction quantized to four bins.
  5. Orientation NMS. Crests are one-sided-tie local maxima along the quantized gradient direction — plateaus thin to 1-px ridges. 28,646 crest cells.
  6. Along-strike depth consensus. Oriented corridor (±800 m strike × ±300 m across): raw count ≥3 and cluster-weight-weighted depth SD ≤445 m. 21,041 cells pass (73.5 %); median depth 770 m; median SD 121 m.
  7. Emission. H8: continuous gaussian(C·γ, σ=1) normalized to [0,1]. H13: binary 1.0 on passing crests. Exact known pixels zeroed; NaN outside the sample footprint.

Assumptions and limits

  • Effective depths are relative to a reference observation plane, not verified ground depths (draped survey datum unverified).
  • Gravity SI=0 on dG/dz is a local top-edge approximation, not a finite-step inversion (Reid & Thurston 2014).
  • A potential-field contact is not automatically a fault; lithologic boundaries and intrusions are confounders.
  • The corridor SD gate uses cluster-weight-weighted moments (pre-scoring correction registered in the preregistration).
