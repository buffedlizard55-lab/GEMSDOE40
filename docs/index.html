01 / A DIFFERENT PHYSICAL SIGNAL

From source depth to a map.

Full method & assumptions ↗
01 — INVERT

Solve for the contact.

Fit Reid’s SI=0 contact equation, including its offset A. A rank-aware solve retains straight contacts without pretending to know their along-strike position.

02 — TEST CONSISTENCY

Keep coherent depths.

12,167 magnetic and 8,450 gravity solutions survive cross-window depth checks. Deep, uncertain and scattered solutions receive less weight.

03 — ESTIMATE DENSITY

Map the point cloud.

Weighted Gaussian KDE becomes a continuous [0,1] confidence field. No gradient threshold, inherited prediction map, top-k dot budget, or random perturbation.
