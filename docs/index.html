    Both proxy columns are monotone in the wrong direction. Combined with the earlier 32-submission correlation of −0.79, this is a replicated finding: predicting the public catalogue is not just uninformative, it is harmful. Two effects survive, and H45 exploits both — the 200 m ring around known faults earns no true-positive credit while still paying the false-positive penalty, and dots should sit on a ~300–600 m lattice because the metric takes a maximum over each 300 m kernel, so neighbours do not add. A forward model fitted to those four scores, DTI = T / (0.2 M + 0.8 G) with G ≈ 5,667 and credit-per-unit-mass 0.0890, reproduces all four to within 2%.
