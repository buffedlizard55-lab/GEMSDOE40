Not Euler — the analytic signal.

Preregistration & amendments ↗
01 — IMAGE EDGES

Amplitude over boundaries.

A = √(Tx²+Ty²+Tz²) peaks over magnetization/density contrasts regardless of magnetization direction (Nabighian 1972), on TMI and on the first vertical gravity derivative.

02 — LABEL DEPTH

Curvature gives depth.

z = √(A/|λ_min(Hessian A)|) — the contact-calibrated local-wavenumber depth (SPI family). Verified on synthetic contacts, dykes and noise before touching real data.

03 — KEEP THE CONSISTENT

Weight, then smooth.

Shallow × persistent-across-continuation × locally dense × depth-consistent × H4-Euler-corroborated cells are Gaussian-KDE-smoothed into a continuous [0,1] field with a soft catalogue-flank ramp.
