## Audit-driven scientific correction

Reid et al. (1990), equation (2) and solution step (3b), explicitly solve for an arbitrary contact offset A at SI=0. The earlier `research_euler.solve_euler_patch` fitted only three position coordinates and forced A=0. A straight contact also does not identify position along its strike; requiring a full-rank 3-D position matrix can reject precisely that geology. These are mathematical/model restrictions, not evidence of an improved competition score. [1](https://www.reid-geophys.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Reid-et-al-1990.pdf)

Gravity is **not** simply interchangeable with magnetics at SI=0. Reid & Thurston correct the finite gravity-step SI to −1 and warn that a finite step needs multiple edges/size parameters. H4 therefore uses SI=0 on the **first vertical derivative of gravity**, explicitly as a local top-edge approximation, not an exact inversion of a finite density step. Raw gravity is not mislabelled as a magnetic SI=0 contact. [2](https://www.reid-geophys.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Reid-Thurston-2014.pdf)

The survey was terrain-draped with variable clearance. All H4 depths are **effective depths below the input grid's reference observation plane**, not verified depths below ground. Upward-continuation height is subtracted from the solved observation-to-source distance. No claim of geothermal permeability, vents, or a reservoir follows from a potential-field contact. [USGS survey metadata](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and)

## H4 frozen generation settings

1. Input hashes: use the existing feature/template/known-mask pins. Discover `tmi` and `iso_grav_anom` by **band_name**, not hardcoded descriptive guesses. Template supplies only shape, CRS, transform, footprint; no template pixel label values used as signal. No prior predictions or proxy labels enter generation.
2. FFT: subtract valid-field mean; nearest-fill invalid cells **only** for derivatives; reflect pad 128 cells. East derivative `+i*kx`, north derivative `-i*krow`, downward derivative `+|k|`. Upward continuation `exp(-|k|h)`. TMI h=200 m, derivative order 0; gravity h=500 m, derivative order 1. Suppress windows within 1,500 m (TMI) / 3,000 m (gravity) of invalid input/rectangular edge. No supplied gradient feature is assumed trustworthy.
3. Windows: TMI 15 and 25 cells, gravity 31 and 51 cells, stride 5 cells for both; all observations in the original grid are used, with no decimation of the window itself. Center coordinates before solving. Use float64 least-squares algebra, with covariance estimated conditionally on each window model.
4. SI=0 contact equation at z=0: `x*Fx + y*Fy = x0*Fx + y0*Fy + z0*Fz + A`. Demean RHS and derivative columns to profile out A. Compute the horizontal gradient second-moment eigenvectors; if minor/major eigenvalue <=0.10, fit normal offset plus depth only and anchor the unidentifiable along-strike coordinate at the window center. Otherwise fit 3-D position. Solve in scaled columns via an eigendecomposition of the small normal matrix (positive singular values); condition number of scaled design <=1,000. Reject zero-energy/rank-deficient remaining depth problems, not straight contacts per se.
5. Acceptance: relative centered fit residual <=0.30, effective depth between 100 and 5,000 m (TMI) or 100 and 8,000 m (gravity), horizontal offset <= half-window side; conditional depth standard error <= max(100 m, 0.25*depth). Report counts rejected by every criterion. Depth uncertainty is an optimistic model-conditional diagnostic, not a posterior including correlated noise/systematic error.
6. Clustering: combine both window scales within each family. Neighbours within 600 m XY and within `max(250 m, 0.35*depth)` in depth, excluding self. Require at least 2 matching neighbours and at least one from the other window scale. A point weight is `exp(-depth/1800) * exp(-(residual/0.30)^2/2) * exp(-(se/max(depth,100)/0.25)^2/2) * n/(n+3) * exp(-(robust_depth_scatter/max(250,0.35*depth))^2/2)`; robust scatter = 1.4826*MAD of matching depths. Saturation and per-family normalization avoid treating overlapping windows as independent discoveries.
7. Rasterize weighted points by bilinear splatting at subpixel XY; Gaussian KDE sigma=2 pixels, truncate=4, constant-zero padding. Separately normalize each family by its positive-cell 99.5th percentile, clipped to [0,1]. Combine as `(M+G+sqrt(M*G))/3`, then rescale by the combined maximum. This is a **continuous** confidence field, not calibrated probability and not a top-k/binary edge map. Shallow coherent clusters receive more mass; unsupported cells remain zero. Suppress **only exact** known-label pixels; do not buffer them. Normalize after this mask, then put NaN exclusively outside the template footprint. Fail if either family has no retained clustered solutions or the output is empty. Never backfill from another submission.
8. Publish the generated cloud as compressed CSV with family, window, XY, effective depth, error, residual, rank and weight. Archive configuration and code hashes. No random noise or timestamp-only difference used to manufacture novelty.
