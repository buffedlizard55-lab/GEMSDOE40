2. **DTI arithmetic** (official metric, α=0.2, β=0.8, R=300 m): marginal mass improves DTI iff its kernel credit k > α·DTI. At owner-best DTI≈0.28 the bar is k>0.056, so *precision of placement* and *false-positive mass control* dominate. The H4-A closure measured that a diffuse field (positive on 43.6% of the footprint) is not spatially selective and loses to random; H8 therefore keeps compact support.
3. **The only organizer-scored lineage improvement measured by the owner** was catalogue-flank pruning (H33-B2: 2,545 pixels at ≤200 m removed from the 0.2708 base → owner-reported 0.2778). Its file↔score attribution is **not** organizer-authenticated, but the direction is consistent with the exact-pixel masking ruling. H8 uses a **soft** flank ramp rather than a hard cut, because staff confirmed adjacent splays remain admissible off the exact pixels.
4. **Instrument honesty.** The frozen 24-block SGMC proxy gate is infeasible (18 wins required, 16 truth-bearing blocks exist) and circular (incumbent is SGMC-derived on SGMC truth). It is retained for diagnostics only. A replacement gate is registered below, explicitly, before any score is read.
5. The analytic-signal amplitude already appears in this repo **only as a window QC gate** (`src/gemsdoe40/euler.py`, "Salem & Ravat 2003 style gating"). No code in this repository has ever used it as a *source-location or depth estimator*, and no local-wavenumber/SPI depth, Werner deconvolution, or scale-persistence filter exists in the inspected corpus. That is the bounded novelty claim; the full prior audit still has to pass pixel-level.

## H8 frozen generation settings (locked before implementation)

1. **Inputs.** Discover `tmi` and `iso_grav_anom` by `band_name`. `labels.tif` supplies the exact known-fault pixel mask and the flank distance field only; no hidden label, proxy pixel, prior prediction, or leaderboard value enters generation. The H4 solution cloud is read from the committed `docs/downloads/h4-euler-solutions.csv.gz` (a GEMSDOE40 artifact, used as corroborating evidence only).
2. **FFT hygiene.** Nearest-fill invalid cells for derivative computation only; subtract the valid-field mean; reflect-pad 128 cells; crop back after every transform (same discipline as frozen H4).
3. **Derivatives.** Horizontal and vertical derivatives are Fourier-domain on the padded grid (east `+i·kx`, north `−i·krow`, downward `+|k|`). **Amendment (registered before any score, replacing the first draft's central-difference/second-derivative mix):** measured synthetic tests showed that untapered Fourier derivatives of contact-like fields carry reflect-pad seam ringing and aliased grid-scale oscillation that corrupt the amplitude curvature; all derivative spectra are therefore multiplied by the taper `exp(−(k/(0.45·k_Nyquist))⁴)`. Documented bias measured on synthetics: depths of z0 ≥ 800 m recovered within ~5%; the shallowest registered depth (300 m) is overshot by ~26% because the taper attenuates the sharpest ridges. The taper is fixed, not tuned per field.
4. **Analytic signal and depth.** `A = sqrt(Tx² + Ty² + Tz²)` with Tx, Ty, Tz from item 3. **Amendment (registered before any score, replacing the Az chain-rule of the first draft):** Fourier second derivatives (`Txz`, `Tyz`, `Tzz`) were implemented and then measured to be dominated by reflect-pad boundary-kink ringing on contact-like fields (synthetic S1 returned systematically wrong depths while first derivatives stayed within ~20%). The depth estimator is therefore the contact-calibrated Hessian form of the same local-wavenumber physics: at an analytic-signal peak, central-difference the Hessian of A, take its most negative eigenvalue λ_min (per m²), and set `z = sqrt(A / |λ_min|)`. For a contact A(ρ) = C·(ρ² + z0²)^(−1/2) this is exactly z0, because λ_min(peak) = −C/z0³. A source whose analytic signal decays as r^(−(n+1)) returns z_true/√(n+1): contact n=0 is exact, pole-like n=1 returns z_true/√2 — the synthetic suite asserts both. The Hessian of A uses only finite differences of the amplitude field after a fixed 1.5-px Gaussian smoothing (registered to stabilize the curvature step against noise; measured clean-depth biases are ~+36% at 300 m, ~+7% at 800 m, ~+2% at 1,500 m, and 2%-noise recovery stays within 30%); no FFT second derivative of T enters. Gravity family: apply the same pipeline to `Gz = F⁻¹[+|k|·F(iso_grav_anom)]` as a local top-edge approximation (Reid & Thurston 2014), exactly as frozen H4 did.
5. **Persistence.** Upward-continue the base field (magnetic: TMI at 0 and 300 m; gravity: Gz at 0 and 500 m) with `exp(-|k|h)`, recompute A and depth, and match height-0 peaks to continued peaks within 3 px XY and `max(400 m, 0.4·z)` depth (continued depth corrected by −h). A matched peak is *persistent*; persistence multiplies the point weight (1.0 persistent, 0.35 otherwise).
6. **Peak selection.** 3×3 non-maximum suppression of A on the height-0 grid, valid cells only, `A ≥ p95` of A over valid cells, and at least 1,500 m (magnetic) / 3,000 m (gravity) from any invalid input cell or the rectangular grid edge. Depth QC: 100–5,000 m (magnetic), 100–8,000 m (gravity). Local consistency: 5×5 median of z within `max(250 m, 0.35·z)`.
7. **Cloud weights.** First draft: per-point `w = exp(−z/1500) · persistence · n/(n+3) · exp(−(1.4826·MAD_z / max(250, 0.35·z))²/2)` over NMS peaks. **Amendment (registered after the first full-grid run and before any score was read):** that sparse construction collapsed — 3,164 magnetic / 4,254 gravity peaks passed QC but NMS peaks sit ~40 px apart, so only 19 + 6 points kept nonzero weight and the output was 1,042 cells, not a fault map. The solution set is therefore the **dense** set of in-footprint cells with depth in bounds and `A ≥ p95(A over in-bound cells)` per family. Depth consistency becomes a local second moment: in a 5×5 window over solution cells, `std_z = sqrt(max(E[z²] − E[z]², 0))`; consistency factor `exp(−0.5·(std_z / max(250 m, 0.35·z))²)`, zero where fewer than 10 of 25 window cells are solutions. Density factor = `count/(count+3)` of solution cells in the same window. Persistence is same-cell: continued amplitude ≥ p95 of continued in-bound amplitudes AND `|z − (z_cont − h)| ≤ max(400 m, 0.4·z)` (factor 1.0 persistent / 0.35 otherwise). Euler corroboration unchanged: same-family H4 solution within 300 m XY and `max(300 m, 0.4·z)` depth multiplies by 2.5. Final weight: `exp(−z/1500) · persistence · density · consistency · corroboration`.
8. **KDE and fusion.** Bilinear-splat weighted points; Gaussian KDE σ=2 px, truncate=3, zero padding. Normalize each family by its positive-cell p99.5, clip to [0,1]; fuse `(M + G + sqrt(M·G))/3`; global p99.5 renormalization.
9. **Catalogue rule.** Exact `labels==1` pixels set to 0; multiply by the flank ramp `clip((d−150 m)/300 m, 0, 1)` where d is Euclidean distance to the nearest label pixel (soft version of the H33 flank prune; frozen before scoring). Support control following the H4-A diffuse-field lesson: values < 0.02 after normalization set to 0; renormalize by the resulting maximum. No top-k budget; support is natural.
10. **Outputs.** Primary research TIFF with NaN outside the footprint; portal-safe all-finite zeros-outside twin plus ZIP; compressed point-cloud CSV; code/config/hash receipts. Deterministic — no random numbers anywhere.

## Synthetic verification plan (ground truth known; run before any real-data score is trusted)

- **S1 contact.** Regional-removed 2-D vertical contact profile `T(x) = C·[atan2(−z0, x−x0) − atan2(−z_reg, x−x0)]` (band-limited like leveled TMI), whose 2-D analytic-signal envelope is the Nabighian (1972) form `|C|/sqrt((x−x0)² + z0²)`; assert recovered peak x within 1 px and depth within 50% at z0 = 300 m (sub-resolution worst case; measured taper+smoothing bias ~+46%) and within 20% for z0 ∈ {800, 1500} m.
- **S2 dyke.** Superposition of two offset contact profiles (edges at x0±w/2, w = 2 km): assert both edge peaks recovered within 3 px and depths within 50% (finite-width dyke bias, measured ~+43%; each edge is only locally contact-like — dykes are not the target class).
- **S3 SI scaling.** Buried pole-like source with analytic-signal decay A ∝ r^(−(n+1)): assert z_est ≈ z_true/√(n+1) — exactly z_true for the n=0 contact and z_true/√2 for the n=1 pole (A ∝ r^(−2)), validating the contact-only calibration and documenting the SI bias.
- **S4 noise.** Add zero-mean noise at 2% of the anomaly range to S1; depth recovery within 30%.
- **S5 footprint discipline.** NaN/invalid-cell handling near masked regions must not create spurious peaks inside the 1,500 m exclusion.
