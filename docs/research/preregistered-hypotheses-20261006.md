# Candidate hypotheses registered before implementation — 2026-10-06 UTC

**Registration status:** hypotheses and decision rules written before any H4 candidate code or output raster is created. This is a dated supplement to the append-only register in [`hypotheses.md`](hypotheses.md); it does not revise the failed H1/H2/H2-B results.

## Decision target and evidence limits

The official challenge asks for geological fault predictions, not geothermal-vent or reservoir locations. Its published metric is the distance-weighted Tversky index with a 300 m triangular kernel, `alpha=0.2`, and `beta=0.8`; the public leaderboard measures only its public label split. The official problem description says the initial prize set consists of expert-labelled faults not present in the existing public database, and that the final prize round uses labels expanded after expert review. Thus a local catalogue-derived holdout is only a proxy and cannot establish hidden-set or final-round performance.

A live leaderboard read on 2026-10-06 showed a top public score of 0.3345. The 0.3195 value in the owner's prompt is not the current top entry in that read. The official leaderboard does not identify a TIFF for each public score. GEMSDOE32's H33-2-B2 page describes the 37,654-pixel file as projected 0.2747 and **UNSCORED**; therefore the official account-level row at 0.2778 cannot be attributed to that file from available evidence.

The local 19-band stack is available in this checkout and is SHA-256 pinned. No new external data is required for these three candidates. A local SGMC-derived raster is available as a proxy only; its provenance is owner-mirrored, not an independently reconstructed official truth source. All proxy metrics must be labelled proxy diagnostics, never organizer scores.

## Ranked, untried hypotheses

### Rank 1 — H4: depth-aware dual-potential-field Euler point-process consensus

**Hypothesis.** Spatially distinct, shallow, low-residual Euler solutions that form compact three-dimensional clusters and agree between magnetic and gravity fields are more fault-like than isolated or deep solutions. Correcting for redundant overlapping windows and requiring one-to-one cross-field support should reduce duplicate votes and false clusters. Projecting the weighted 3-D solution cloud through a compact 2-D KDE should create the required continuous per-pixel prediction field.

- **Layers:** `rtp` (magnetic; band 2) and `iso_grav_anom` (gravity; band 13) from the supplied multiband GeoTIFF. The supplied gravity vertical-gradient layer is not substituted for raw gravity.
- **Physical signature:** depth-labelled Euler source points, not thresholded gradient magnitude. Magnetic SI is fixed at 0 for an idealized deep contact. The gravity model is fixed at SI = −1 as the published correction for an idealized finite gravity step; this is an approximation within ordinary single-window Euler, not a complete generalized finite-step inversion. Reid & Thurston (2014) explicitly warn that a finite gravity step needs a more generalized formulation, so H4 must report that model limitation and cannot claim a uniquely correct gravity SI for real faults.
- **Frozen implementation:** fill nodata only for derivative calculation; compute downward-positive `+|k|` Fourier vertical derivatives on a 128-cell reflect-padded field and central finite-difference horizontal derivatives at the 100 m cell size; apply no upward continuation or low-pass filter. Solve 10×10-pixel Euler windows on a 4-pixel lattice (sampled 3×3 systems); retain only active normal-matrix condition number ≤1e5 (3×3 for SI=0, 4×4 for SI=−1), depths 100–2,500 m, normalized least-squares residual `sqrt(SSE)/sqrt(sum(rhs²)) ≤0.20`, and source offsets within five pixels. Collapse redundant points to the highest-base-weight solution per 200 m × 200 m × 200 m source voxel. For each remaining point, count its own field-family neighbours within 300 m horizontal distance and 300 m absolute depth; require at least three points including itself. The local cluster factor is `min(n/3, 1) × exp(−depth_MAD/150 m)`, where depth MAD is the median absolute deviation of the neighbours' source depths. Base weight is `exp(−depth/900 m) × exp[−0.5(residual/0.20)²] × exp[−0.5(offset_px/2)²]`; field weight is base × cluster factor. Accept only mutual-nearest magnetic–gravity pairs under the fixed squared distance `(dx²+dy²)/300² + dz²/300²`, and additionally require horizontal distance ≤300 m and absolute depth difference ≤300 m. Pair weight is the geometric mean of the two field weights times `exp[−0.5((dXY/300)²+(dZ/300)²)]`. Bilinearly deposit the pair cloud; use a 1.5-pixel Gaussian KDE truncated at 3σ; normalize by the in-footprint maximum to [0,1]; zero only exact known-label pixels; preserve NaN only outside the sample footprint. No proxy values, holdout scores, or leaderboard outcomes enter the construction. If the locked gates produce no positive pair support, stop without relaxing gates or substituting a gradient map.
- **Why it can identify faults missing from USGS/INGENIOUS:** it seeks subsurface contacts that jointly perturb susceptibility and density, including buried or eroded traces; it suppresses exact known-label pixels and does not draw known faults as predictions. Such a contact is not proof of a fault: lithologic boundaries, intrusions, alteration, and non-tectonic density changes are confounders.
- **Difference from this repository:** H1 used a 2-D one-neighbour gravity/magnetic match with SI=0 on a derived gravity vertical-derivative field and stopped with zero accepted gravity solutions; H2/H2-B used single-field TMI persistence across upward-continuation heights; H40-1/H40-4 used 2-D field KDE/crest or multiscale conjunction. H4 instead uses the raw gravity field with an explicitly caveated gravity SI, de-duplicates overlapping-window solutions in 3-D source voxels, requires mutual one-to-one 3-D cross-family matches, and projects the matched cloud only after 3-D weighting. This is a method-level distinction, not a novelty claim relative to all external research.
- **Expected DTI effect / cost:** rank 1; moderate positive prior relative to single-field Euler because it requires two physical channels, but high uncertainty from gravity-model mismatch and lithologic confounding. Implementation cost: medium-high (derivatives, two windowed inversions, 3-D voxelization/matching/KDE, full audit). No external data required.

### Rank 2 — H5: conductive alteration-front / basement-structure coincidence

**Hypothesis.** The edge of a shallow electrical-conductivity anomaly that coincides with a basement-depth break and a potential-field contact is more likely to reflect a fault-controlled hydrothermal alteration zone than a conductivity anomaly alone.

- **Layers:** `cond_surf` (band 17), `depth_to_base_surf` (band 15), plus `rtp` (band 2) and `iso_grav_anom` (band 13) as independent structural corroboration.
- **Physical signature:** a conductivity boundary and abrupt basement-depth gradient that overlap a magnetic/gravity source-contact corridor; do not label the conductor itself as a vent or fault.
- **Why it can identify faults missing from the catalogue:** conductive alteration and basin-fill/basement offsets can mark buried fluid pathways with no mapped surface scarp. The coincidence condition is intended to avoid emitting every regional conductor or lithologic boundary.
- **Difference from this repository:** prior implementations use Euler density, magnetic/gravity edges, terrain/scarp, or catalogue flanks; no current code jointly tests a conductivity-front × basement-depth break × potential-field contact interaction.
- **Expected DTI effect / cost:** rank 2; low-to-moderate expected gain with high geological confounding (clay, salinity, basin fill, and lithology also affect conductivity). Cost: medium. Inputs are already in the downloaded stack; no external data required.

### Rank 3 — H6: geodetic strain-release relays with seismic corroboration

**Hypothesis.** Unmapped relay zones between mapped structures are more likely to host faults when localized shear and dilatation anomalies co-occur with nearby earthquake evidence, rather than when any single strain or seismic band is high.

- **Layers:** `geod_shearrate` (band 7), `geod_dilaterate` (band 8), `geod_2ndinv` (band 4), and `deq_n100a15` / `ieq_n100a15` (bands 10/16), with `existing_faults` used only for exact-label exclusion and spatial folds.
- **Physical signature:** a scale-localized shear/dilatation saddle or gradient-divergence zone supported by the supplied earthquake context; candidate relays are placed between, not directly on, the known catalogue traces.
- **Why it can identify faults missing from the catalogue:** strain transfer and seismicity can reveal active or reactivated structures whose surface expression is subtle or incomplete in a static fault compilation.
- **Difference from this repository:** current models include earthquake distance/density and geodetic bands as features but do not test a preregistered multi-layer relay geometry with blockwise holdout and an exact-label exclusion.
- **Expected DTI effect / cost:** rank 3; low-to-moderate and spatially limited expected gain because the geodetic grids may be smoothed and the event-density bands are indirect. Cost: medium. All input layers are already present; no external data required.

## Append-only outcome and fallback registration — 2026-10-06 UTC

### H4 feasibility result (before scoring or prior-raster comparison)

H4 generated **no candidate GeoTIFF** and did not read the proxy. The locked SI=0 `rtp` inversion returned 3,237 accepted magnetic solutions. For raw SI=−1 `iso_grav_anom`, 316,339 windows passed the input-validity screen; only 24 met the preregistered normal-matrix condition limit, all 24 met the relative-fit and source-offset checks, but **zero** produced a depth in the locked 100–2,500 m interval. Thus the dual-field mutual-nearest cloud could not be formed. The threshold and physical gates were not relaxed. H4 is recorded as a feasibility stop, not a score or an output candidate.

### Rank 4 — H7: gravity-gradient-weighted magnetic Euler source cloud (registered before H7 code)

**Reason for a separate fallback:** H4's raw gravity SI=−1 solve is infeasible under its locked condition/depth gates. H7 does not pretend that this missing gravity Euler cloud exists; it makes a different, explicitly lower-confidence geological claim.

- **Layers:** `rtp` (band 2) for Euler locations/depths, and raw `iso_grav_anom` (band 13) for a continuous gravity-contact context weight. No external data.
- **Physical signature:** accepted shallow SI=0 magnetic source solutions form depth-consistent 3-D clusters; solutions colocated with stronger gravity horizontal-gradient contrast receive a bounded soft weight, not a binary threshold. Potentially unmapped lithologic contacts remain a confounder.
- **Frozen implementation:** use H4's exact magnetic solver and gates (10×10 window, 4-pixel stride; active normal-matrix condition ≤1e5; depth 100–2,500 m; normalized residual ≤0.20; source offset ≤5 px); deduplicate at one highest-weight 200 m × 200 m × 200 m source voxel; require at least three same-field solutions including self within 300 m XY and 300 m depth; cluster factor `min(n/3,1) × exp(−depth_MAD/150 m)`; base shallowness/fit/offset weight unchanged. Compute gravity horizontal-gradient magnitude from band 13 using the same 100 m east/north finite differences; calculate each valid in-footprint cell's empirical percentile rank with average ranks for ties; retain a magnetic solution for H7 only when its nearest source pixel lies inside the sample footprint and has valid gravity context; assign each retained source the nearest-cell multiplier `0.5 + 0.5 × rank`. Multiply that factor into the point base weight **before** voxel deduplication and cluster weighting. Bilinearly deposit weighted source coordinates; apply Gaussian KDE σ=1.5 px, truncate=3σ; normalize by in-footprint maximum; zero exact known-label pixels only; NaN outside. No gravity threshold, holdout value, leaderboard value, or proxy enters construction. If no positive clustered support remains, stop; do not relax thresholds.
- **Why it may find catalogue-missing faults:** magnetic contact Euler locations and depth clustering target buried structures; the independently measured gravity gradient only increases or decreases the confidence rather than drawing lineaments itself. The method does not claim that a gravity edge proves a fault or geothermal productivity.
- **Difference from repository methods:** unlike H4, H7 does not require an unsupported raw-gravity Euler source or one-to-one depth match; unlike H1 it does not use a second vertical derivative of gravity in an Euler solve; unlike H2-B it is a spatially deduplicated 3-D source-cloud KDE, not four-height TMI persistence. It remains within the repository's established Euler family and is not claimed novel relative to all public research.
- **Expected DTI / cost:** lower confidence than H4's intended independent depth consensus; low-to-moderate expected change versus a single-field Euler map because the gravity modifier is bounded to `[0.5,1]`. Cost: medium. All bands already exist locally; no external data dependency.
- **Validation:** same 24 spatial blocks, three-cell guard, current proxy pin and full refreshed output corpus; no weekly slot unless it beats the highest comparable prior under the frozen 18/24 + −0.005 block gates, exact-grid format audit, and raw/pixel uniqueness checks. A failed score is research-only.
